# Enclosure & Mechanical Mounts

This directory contains CAD models, STL files, and mechanical design notes for the robot chassis and sensor brackets.

## Planned Mounts

1. **Front Ultrasonic Bracket**:
   - Tri-sensor mount holding Left (-45°), Center (0°), and Right (+45°) HC-SR04/RCWL-1601 sensors.
   - Elevation: ~6-8 cm above ground level.

2. **Low-Profile Front IR Bumper**:
   - Holds 2x digital IR sensors angled downward/forward to catch low obstacles or drop-offs.
   - Elevation: ~1.5-2 cm above ground level.

3. **Flank & Rear IR Brackets**:
   - Midpoint chassis clips for Side-Left and Side-Right sensors.
   - Rear clip for Rear-Center sensor protecting reverse maneuvers.

4. **Electronics & Power Deck**:
   - Standoff plate for ESP32 Dev Board and motor driver.
   - Elastic or cradle mount for USB-C power bank.
